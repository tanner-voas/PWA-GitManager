Profile observations capture initiating profile, day, and writer; local cumulative counts merge by maximum per writer and field. Shared requests stay account-wide. Translation counts known provider responses, speech counts attempts and cache hits, Drive counts observed profile-specific calls, not quota units. Regular Drive backup includes profile snapshots with the existing rolling 31-day horizon. Unlinked local observations remain local rather than silently assigning them to a later account. Local storage failures, browser closure, and bounds may limit completeness. No new restore mechanism. Sync is best effort while active.
